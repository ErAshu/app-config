# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 24, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **2048 Game** handles information when you use the app.

## Information We Collect

2048 Game is designed to be played without creating an account.

The app does not directly collect, store, or sell personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

Game progress, scores, settings, and other gameplay preferences may be stored locally on your device to provide core app functionality.

## Game Center

2048 Game supports Apple Game Center features, including leaderboards for different board sizes.

If you choose to use Game Center, Apple may process information related to your gameplay, such as your scores, achievements, Game Center nickname, avatar, and other Game Center activity. This information is handled by Apple according to Apple's privacy practices.

Your Game Center nickname and leaderboard scores may be visible to other Game Center users, depending on your Game Center settings and Apple's services.

For more information, please review Apple's Game Center privacy information.

## How Information Is Used

Information stored locally on your device may be used to:

- Save your game progress and preferences
- Remember your best scores
- Track completed challenges and daily streaks
- Provide rewards and gameplay features

We do not use your information for advertising or marketing purposes.

## Data Sharing

2048 Game does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Except for information processed through Apple services such as Game Center when you choose to use those features, we do not share personal information with third parties.

## Data Storage

Gameplay data stored locally on your device remains under your control. You may be able to remove locally stored app data by deleting the app or using available device settings.

Game Center information is managed by Apple and is subject to Apple's applicable policies and account settings.

## Children's Privacy

2048 Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that personal information has been provided to us through the app, please contact us so that we can review the request.

## Third-Party Services

The app uses Apple Game Center to provide leaderboard functionality.

Apple's use of information is governed by Apple's own privacy policies and terms.

If additional third-party services, such as advertising, analytics, crash reporting, cloud storage, or payment services, are added in a future version of the app, this Privacy Policy will be updated as necessary.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app or applicable requirements.

Any updates will be posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be revised.
